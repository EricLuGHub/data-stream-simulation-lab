# Trial 14: Passive Hybrid

Approach:
- Slow EMA and conservative hybrid fair value.
- Wider quoting and smaller trade sizes.
- Only takes obvious edge and only improves quotes when it is clearly safe.
- Strong inventory skew, but limited aggression on both sides.

Why this one is passive:
- It is designed to make money mostly by collecting spread and avoiding bad fills, rather than chasing edge aggressively.
